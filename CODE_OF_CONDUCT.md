# Code of Conduct

NRVL Network follows the **[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)**. It applies to everyone taking part in this project — in issues, pull requests, discussions and anywhere else the project is represented.

## In short

- Be respectful and considerate. Assume good intent.
- Welcome newcomers and differing experience levels.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- No harassment, insults, discriminatory language, or publishing of others' private information.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, contact the maintainer, [@princenrvl](https://github.com/princenrvl), privately through GitHub. Reports are handled confidentially.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may remove or edit contributions that breach this code and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors, following the enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant.
